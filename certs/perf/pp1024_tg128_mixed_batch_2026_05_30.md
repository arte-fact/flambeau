## Findings

1. **MoE benefit holds on dephased shape**: 35B-A3B pp2tp2 dephased gets +3.7 %–+5.0 % cumulated decode throughput with all consumers individually faster. Not the +14.4 % from the K5 sustained cert (only 4 streams × 10 s experiment vs 30+12 = 42 events in K5), but the direction and per-cohort pattern match.
2. **Gain scales with overlap density**: tighter stagger (1.0 s vs 2.0 s) doubles the cumulated improvement (+5.0 % vs +3.7 %) because more time is spent in concurrent-decode + new-prefill regime where mixed engagement can fire.
3. **Dense 27B sees no gain on this workload shape**: pp2tp2 +0.8 %, PP4 +1.2 % — both within noise. The dense FFN can't amortise expert-sort the way MoE does.
4. **TTFT essentially unchanged ON vs OFF on every config**: mixed-batch's lever is on decode-step amortisation during the prefill chunk, not on first-chunk TTFT.
5. **PP4 27B is structurally slower than pp2tp2 27B** (over-wall 17 vs 25 tok/s; TTFT 2.1-2.7 s vs 1.3-1.5 s) — confirms the production-topology choice. PP4 has no intra-layer compute parallelism.

## Production recommendation (refined from K5)

- **35B-A3B / pp2tp2 + `FLAMBEAU_MIXED_BATCH=1`**: free win on dephased + sustained workloads alike. Tighter overlap regimes get more benefit.
- **27B / pp2tp2**: K5 sustained cert showed long-prompt p50 wall −44 % at the cost of short p50 +43 % (latency-redistribution lever). On this dephased pp1024/tg128 shape the gain is within noise. ON if RAG/summarisation-dominated, OFF for short-only chat.
- **PP4 anything**: not worth running for production traffic. pp2tp2 is uniformly better on the 4-MI50 rig.
